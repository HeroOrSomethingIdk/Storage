Unit 13.

Solace and Korrin sat beside each other, sharing a single pair of earphones as Solace's Nightcore playlist blasted quietly between them. Korrin had only recently warmed up to the genre — mostly because it's all what Solace would play.

"I won't fall down, you need me~" Solace sang softly along with the track, her head bobbing side to side in perfect rhythm.

Korrin chuckled under his breath, watching her vibe without a single care for anything happening around them. Her energy was contagious — he couldn't help smiling too.

Neither of them noticed the pair of optics watching from the shadows.

Zephyr had been hiding in the dark corner of the room, studying them. Observing. His corrupted claws scraped lightly across the floor as he shifted closer.

Solace was bright. Radiating a toxic amount of positive mood that felt almost... supernatural. Korrin smiled. Solace smiled. The air around them was warm, easy, alive.

He wanted that. He wanted to feel that kind of happiness.

Slowly, silently, Zephyr crept toward them — claws dragging faint lines into the metal floor with each predatory step.

"I'm not done believe me~" Solace continued when suddenly—

The music cut off.

"Korrin!" Solace pouted.

"It's not me!" He raised his hands defensively as both of them turned toward the culprit.

Zephyr stood there, claws wrapped around Solace's phone, inspecting it like an unknown artifact.

"Zephyr? What are you doing?" Solace tilted her head.

Zephyr snarled softly. "This device is foreign to me. Tell me — what does it do?"

"Oh, it's just my phone." Solace reached forward and snatched it gently before he could crush it with a twitch. "It provides me with communications, and entertainment."

Her fingers tapped across the screen.

"Here." She restarted the song she had been playing before he interrupted it.

Zephyr sat down — almost like a dog —tilting his head at the strange mix of beats and sped-up vocals.

"It's music!" Solace declared proudly.

"Fascinating. I don't understand it at all," Zephyr said, optics narrowing as if trying to analyze the noise.

Solace shot Korrin a look that silently begged for help.

"Maybe you just don't like this genre," Korrin said, taking the phone. "Believe me, I get it. Solace only plays Nightcore. I haven't heard anything else in months."

"Hey! Nightcore is objectively the best genre out there!" Solace complained.

Korrin ignored her and tapped the screen.

A smooth jazz track began to play — soft sax, low bass, gentle rhythms.

Zephyr froze.

Then slowly, his posture shifted. He leaned closer to the phone, almost entranced.

"This tone... is actually quite enjoyable." he murmured.

He lowered himself fully to the floor, relaxing as his tail flicked back and forth like an entertained cat.

For the first time ever, Solace's phone was playing something other than Nightcore.

"You like it?" Solace asked with hope in her voice.

"It's very... entertaining to listen to." Zephyr replied, voice oddly calm.

"Great! You can have my phone for now." Solace handed it to him and turned to Korrin. "Come on, let's get something to eat!"

"Heh, you read my mind." Korrin said, standing as the duo walked off together.

Zephyr barely noticed them leave.  
He was already immersed — scrolling through the wide library of music now available to him, listening intently like he had just discovered a new sense.

The music had ease his internal tensions he was feeling in his own body. The constant pain he was feeling from his body keeping itself together from falling apart cleared his usually distorted mind.

The peace has caused him to linger to the recent memories he has scanned. But one memory in particular stood out.
