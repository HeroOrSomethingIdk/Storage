Act 2: Revolution – Epilogue

V sat alone in the quiet of Unit 12, Solace resting peacefully in her arms. The soft hum of the night contrasted sharply with the weight in her chest. As she looked down at her daughter, she felt the absence of A more sharply than ever.

She stayed there long into the night, rocking Solace gently, letting the memories of A fill her heart. And with each beat, she vowed silently: she would honor his life, his love, and his sacrifice-no matter what the future held.
