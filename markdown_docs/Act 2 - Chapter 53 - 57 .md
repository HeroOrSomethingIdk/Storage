Rin finally spoke, her tone clipped, official. "A lot has happened since you were gone. We learned to manage without you."

For the first time in years, Yeva didn't know how to approach a battlefield.

But not as a mother.

Chapter 54:

A's rocket boots made him move at breakneck speed, as he cleaves through XR's core in a single slice.

Her optics widened, she staggered once, reaching out as if in disbelief... and then collapsed, her frame sparking and motionless.

"Its over..." V whispered.

But for tonight, Unit 13 belonged to them.
