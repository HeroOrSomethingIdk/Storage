=== 3 years after Unit 12's massacre ===

Around the blizzards of Copper-9 stood the most fortified worker drone colonies of all: Unit 6.

A research facility that contained technology researched specifically lost in Copper-9, but after the extinction of humanity, it became a colony for worker drones.

However, the company has been attempting to raid this facility for years, and not a single squad was ever successful.

"Murder drones incoming!" A worker drone with military-grade armor yelled.

The entrance of Unit 6 was heavily fortified. Worker drones armed with military-grade rifles, sentry guns that would shred disassembly drones in seconds.

Pillboxes armed with heavy machine guns, and a blast door that had tanked slashes, gunshots, and even explosions from disassembly drones without a single scratch-a door built to perfection.

"Man your stations!" A tall worker said, placing a magazine in his heavily customized M4A1.

He wore a gray jacket, black boots, and a blue construction helmet with a pair of red safety goggles.

"No one gets past my doors." He continued.

From the heavens, squads of disassembly drones rush in from all directions, wings flared—cannons, guns, and claws ready with killing intent.

"FIRE!" The tall drone commanded.

In a few seconds, gun shots from sentry guns and worker drone rifles lit up the entire facility.

Disassembly drones being shredded by bullets in record speed.

One disassembly drone however wasn't taken down so easily.

The commander of the assault drove from the sky in blitz speed, entering a pillbox and instantly massacred everyone inside.

"We got casualties Khan! The commander is too fast for us to shoot!" A worker drone reported.

Khan gave the reporting drone a determined look and nodded. He walks down to the battlefield with a commanding presence.

As the disassembly drones and the defenses continue their skirmishes, Khan stepped forward and faced the commander head on.

The commander looked at Khan, claws holding a dead worker drone. He threw him aside then fully faced Khan, confidence radiating as he smirked.

"So you're the leader of this bunker? How brave, the leader showing up in the front lines." He grinned, claws twitching.

"You must have a death wish." He continued.

"No, not really. I just wish you didn't kill all those worker drones. They had families you know—children expecting them to come home." Khan replied.

"I'm here to get rid of you." Khan declared, lifting his rifle on his shoulder.

"Then die." The commander said—immediately charging with his blade.

Khan barely dodged the strike, the commander following the attack up with a claw strike.

He rolls to evade the attack then starts running away.

"What? Running already? The commander mocked, wings flaring—chasing after Khan.

Khan turned his back, then threw a device on the floor--it immediately expanded into an instant door that the commander crashed into.

"Ouch—what the?" the commander growled in confusion when the door slammed open harshly-revealing Khan already aiming his gun.

He didn't spare him a word. He unloaded his entire mag, the bullets penetrating through his frame.

Eventually, his rifle clicked empty. The commander staggered, but was alive.

He let out a sadistic laugh—oil dripping from his frame. "You're so paying for that." he growled, claws twitching-ready to kill.

Khan's eyes widened as he ran as fast as he could. Panicking, he reached a safety bunker and desperately tried to close the door.

He was too late. The commander entered.
"Now you're stuck in this bunker with me." The commander said with a sadistic grin.

"No." Khan said, arms folded-shaking his head. "You're stuck in a room with me." He continued as the lights turned on—revealing a dozen sentry guns pointing at the commander.

"Murder drones are nothing but animals-always chasing for the kill. Predictable." Khan said coldly.

With that, the sentry guns fired at the commander—tearing him apart as he fell limp to the ground.

Khan simply turned and walked away from the kill chamber.

=== A day later, outskirts of Unit 6 ===

"Heavily armed guards, and fortified structures-this place is the real deal." X said, binoculars on hand.

"The others are in position, our job is to breach that door." J started, pointing to the indestructible blast door.

From the side, Serial Designation Z joined the conversation. A male disassembly drone with a blank trench coat, and a black fedora.

"I will provide gun support from above." He said quietly.

While the two squads strategize, discussing positioning, roles and timings—N's attention drifted to Unit 6.

There, he spotted it.

A fatal flaw with the defense.

The door was indestructible, but faint hints of thick power cables visible under the plating of the entrance.

All he had to do is rip it apart and the door will falter.

"If I do this myself, then maybe... Maybe J will respect me again." N muttered to himself.

N unfolds his wings, claws ready.

"What the? N! Don't you dare!" J shouted, reaching out for him in an attempt to stop him.

"Sorry J, I'll be right back!" N soared to the sky then rushed towards the blast door.

"MURDER DRONE!!!" A worker drone shouted as the alarms started sounding.

"N!" V shouted, ready to help him until J held V's shoulder to stop her.

"J??? N needs our help! He'll die out there!" V argued.

N moved like a storm. Diving through the heavy fortifications of Unit 6 through heavy gun fire like it was nothing.

"Come on N, show them you're useful for once. No more hesitating!"

He lifted his claws forward, blocking the bullets that were flying at him. He dodged as best he could, spinning, moving in zigzags—any motion to avoid getting hit.

He landed near a group of worker drones and, in the next moment, decapitated them all at once with his blade.

A worker drone fired a rocket from a nearby watchtower. N reacted quickly—kicking it back, returning to sender.

The rocket caused a massive explosion behind N as he grabbed the panel on the floor with his claws and ripped it off with ease—revealing the thick power lines keeping the door shut.

He stabbed the wires with his tail, injecting all his nanite acid reserves as they melted.

Within seconds, the door opened, revealing worker drones frozen in place.

"Heya fellas." N greeted as he rushed and disassembled everything within the area.

In a minute, he has slaughtered all worker drones and destroyed all sentry guns stationed by the entrance of Unit 6.

For the first time since the construction of the impenetrable door, Unit 6 had been breached.

All done by N alone.

From behind, footsteps of disassembly drones echo towards him.

"Woah N." V said in disbelief.

Behind him was V, and J.

J stepped forward, eyes wide.

"N..." Her voice carried genuine disbelief. "No squad has ever breached Unit 6. And yet you—" She paused, then nodded once. "Good work."

Three more disassembly drones land right by them.

"What a brilliant performance you put out there N, I couldn't have done a better job myself." Y gave an exaggerated bow, genuinely impressed.

"I told you N would surprise you one day." X said to J, smirking.

He straightened, wiping oil from his visor with a shaky laugh. "Ha! See? I told you guys I could be useful! Just took... you know... a little finesse!"

V stepped forward, placing a hand on N's shoulder. "Good job N."

"I'll let command know of your outstanding performance. I'm proud of you N." J said.

For the first time in years, N didn't feel useless. He didn't feel the guilt he had carried for his poor performance as a disassembly drone.

For the first time, he got the validation he was craving for.

"Our job is not finished." Z said quietly.

"Right. Y, Z, you both come with me" X commanded. "J, wipe out the other side. Let's finally give these scraps the beating they deserve." He continued.

J nodded, then faced her squad. "You two, with me."

"Yes ma'am!" N said, giving an exaggerated salute.

Hours later, J stood before the Company overseers. Her frame was damaged, but intact enough that she could stand.

"Unit 6 is annihilated." she reported flatly. "The bunker is no more."

A pause. Then the overseer's voice crackled coldly through the comms.

"Where is the rest of your squad?"

"Lost..." J admitted, her visor flickering in shame.

Heavy silence followed, then the overseer spoke.

"Unacceptable. But... the mission was a success, and given it was your squad that breached the previously unbreakable defense of Unit 6, we will let this slide. You will continue serving, Commander J. Do not fail us again."

J stood there, empty.

She turns back, facing where N and V would have been standing. Instead, no one was there.

V, gone. N, gone. Even the purple worker drone had slipped from her grasp.

Her hands trembled until she felt a familiar presence.

"J."

X approached slowly, his expression soft beneath his visor.

The sight of him broke the last of her composure. J collapsed against him, claws clutching his sleeves, her voice breaking into tears.

"They left me, X. My squad... my family. They chose a worker drone over me. Was it my fault? Did I push them away?"

X's arms wrapped around her firmly, grounding her.

"No. You did everything you could. You were strong when they weren't. You held the line."

J's sobs shook against him. For once, the ruthless commander was just broken-and only X remained to hold her together.

=== Meanwhile, in the wilderness ===

N, V and Uzi staggered into the wastelands beyond Unit 6.

They found shelter in a cave. They decided to rest for the night.

Uzi sat near the back, hugging her legs as exhaustion and grief weighed heavily on her.

Her father's last words echoed repeatedly in her processors as tears started flowing down again.

N sat beside her carefully, keeping a respectful distance. His usually cheerful tone was gentler now.

"I'm... I'm sorry. About your dad. He was... brave."

Uzi's visor flickered. She clenched her fist tighter. "He shouldn't have died... he shouldn't have had to."

"I know." N said softly. "But he loved you. That's why he fought. And... because of him, I saw the truth. He saved more than just you. He saved me, too."

Uzi looked at him-not as an enemy, but as someone who understood her.

A friend.

She sat in silence, processing everything that had happened.

In the span of a single day, everything had been taken from her. Her family. Her colony. Her home.

V stood silently near the cave entrance, her arms crossed, one foot against the wall.

She remained silent. Her mind replayed all the massacres she had done knowing now that they were all people. They were all alive.

"We were wrong." she muttered to herself. "All of us."

The three sat in the silence of the cave-broken and grieving, yet finally free.

But freedom had come with a price.

And the cost was everything.
